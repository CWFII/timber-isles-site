Timber Isles Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the Timber Isles mobile game ("the App") handles information. The App is published by Craig Felos ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email, or other personal information. Game progress (islands, items, buildings, coins and purchased upgrades) is saved only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional reward ads you choose to watch. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and app interaction data to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy. On iPhone, the App asks for permission before using your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still get the full game and see non-personalized ads. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In-app purchases (Apple App Store)

The App offers optional purchases (such as bag slots, Auto Eat, Auto Sell, coin packs, ad skips and Remove Ads) processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your card or billing information. We only receive confirmation of the purchase so the App can unlock the item. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page.
